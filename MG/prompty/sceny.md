# Sceny i lokacje

[← Spis promptów](README.md)

Każdy prompt zacznij od **stylu wspólnego** z [postacie.md](postacie.md#styl-wspólny-wklejaj-na-początku-każdego-promptu). Jeśli chcesz, żeby na scenie byli bohaterowie, dopisz na końcu ich opisy (pełne albo same rdzenie) w miejsce znacznika `[BOHATEROWIE]`. Bez bohaterów obrazek sprawdzi się jako tło lokacji.

Kolumna **Kiedy pokazać** mówi, od którego momentu grafika nie zdradza niczego, czego gracze jeszcze nie wiedzą.

## 1. Akademia z zewnątrz

```text
A gnomish academy of magic built on the ocean floor, a cluster of brass towers, round portholes and copper pipes under a huge glowing glass dome that holds back the dark sea. Schools of fish, kelp forests, bioluminescent jellyfish, warm golden light leaking from every window. Night, deep blue water, a sense of wonder and slight fragility, tiny cracks of light flickering on the barrier.
```

## 2. Atrium po alarmie

```text
A grand circular atrium under a glass dome, the dark ocean outside with a school of fish pressed against the glass. In the center a golden statue of four heroic gnome students stands crooked on a wheeled dolly. Water drips from the ceiling into puddles, red alarm lights flash, a fake glued-on beard lies abandoned on the polished floor. [BOHATEROWIE] arrive in a hurry, looking alarmed.
```

## 3. Dyżurka profesora Plinka

```text
A tiny cramped night-watch office the size of a closet, smelling of tea and ozone. A wall of blinking indicator lights in green and yellow with one red light pulsing ominously. A hex key hangs on a hook with a keychain shaped like a crossed-out frog. An elderly gnome professor with huge spectacles and a nervous smile frantically fills out paperwork, surrounded by towers of forms and rubber stamps.
```

## 4. Biblioteka i Vellum

```text
A towering restricted section of a gnomish library, shelves up to a vaulted ceiling, ladders on rails, a fine old carpet soaked with sea water. A stern elderly gnome librarian with a tall grey bun and a finger to her lips is whisper-shouting, holding up a rolled blueprint. Floating candles, dripping water, books lifted onto high shelves to keep them dry. [BOHATEROWIE] stand guiltily in the puddle.
```

## 5. Korytarz B i „studenci”

```text
A narrow corridor of an underwater school lined with copper pipes and cheerful safety posters featuring a smiling gnome woman. Puddles on the floor. A group of four small reptilian creatures with dog-like snouts and scaly tails poorly disguised as gnome students: crooked glued-on beards, oversized student robes with sleeves far too long, noses extended with corks, marching in perfect lockstep with nervous grins. Comedic tension. [BOHATEROWIE] watch them with deep suspicion.
```

## 6. Kotłownia i Splot

```text
A steamy boiler room beneath the academy, huge brass boilers, valves and gauges, three thick bundles of cables wrapped in tape, warm orange light and drifting steam. A small mischievous blue-grey sprite in tiny maintenance overalls swings on a valve wheel, holding a scorecard and looking unimpressed. Pipes leak little jets of water. [BOHATEROWIE] work on the cables with tape and wrenches.
```

## 7. Magazyn kryształów

```text
A storage room full of metal crates and labelled shelves of glowing resonance crystals in amber and blue, each in its own padded socket. One socket on the central shelf is conspicuously empty. A suspicious paint trap rigged above the door with a bucket of bright yellow paint. Cold blue light, dust motes, a sense of something missing.
```

## 8. Wieża Pieczęci

```text
A narrow tower at the heart of the academy, a large control board with sliding tokens arranged in a triangle, two massive copper rods crackling with blue electricity, and an enormous red button on a pedestal like a monument to bad decisions. The air shimmers with ozone. An old gnome with a mug of tea stands nearby with the knowing face of someone who has been through this before. [BOHATEROWIE] study the board.
```

## 9. Ożywione narzędzia

```text
A chaotic fight in a boiler room: wrenches, hammers, pliers and a mop have come to life and attack with comic fury, hopping and snapping like angry animals. Sparks, flying bolts, steam. [BOHATEROWIE] defend themselves with spells and a big adjustable wrench. Dynamic action composition, slapstick energy.
```

## 10. Wodny żywiołak-stażysta

```text
A low flooded crawlspace beneath the atrium floor, a leak spraying like a badly designed fountain. A small water elemental made of swirling sea water rises from the flood, wearing a tiny intern badge and holding a soggy safety form, looking eager and confused rather than hostile. Teal and silver light reflecting on the low ceiling. [BOHATEROWIE] crouch at the edge, unsure whether to fight or fill in the form.
```

## 11. Szept posągu

```text
Close-up of the golden statue of four gnome heroes in the dim atrium, one figure in the statue glowing faintly from within, its eyes softly lit, as if whispering. Water ripples on the floor reflect golden light. Quiet, emotional, slightly eerie mood in an otherwise funny story.
```

## 12. Pokój gościnny

```text
A guest room for exchange students turned into a disguise workshop: piles of fake beards, glue pots, face paint for freckles, cork noses, an attendance list pinned to the wall, a sack marked with a big coin symbol. A motivational poster with a cartoon dragon hangs crookedly. A round window looks out onto a metal transport shaft. Messy, funny, incriminating.
```

## 13. Kapitan Klik na wózku

```text
A small cunning reptilian creature with a dog-like snout and a bottle-cap captain badge stands heroically on a wheeled dolly carrying a golden gnome statue, steering it like a war chariot toward a metal transport shaft. Another small reptilian creature in a lopsided fake beard steers with a mop handle. Sirens, flashing lights, water spraying. [BOHATEROWIE] give chase. Dynamic comedic chase scene, motion blur.
```

## 14. Epilog u smoka

```text
A young dragon with gleaming scales sits in a modest cave lair on top of a disappointingly small pile of coins, proudly hugging a life-size cardboard statue of four gnomes painted gold, completely convinced it is real. A letter lies at its feet. Warm torchlight, comedic satisfied expression, motivational posters on the cave walls.
```

Kolor smoka dobierz do wersji z [../opisy/smok-i-koboldy.md](../opisy/smok-i-koboldy.md): dopisz `green` albo `brass` przed słowem `dragon`.
