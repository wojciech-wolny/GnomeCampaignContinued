# Bohaterowie — wygląd i portrety

[← Spis promptów](README.md)

Opisy powstały na podstawie pola „Wygląd” z kart postaci i historii bohaterów. Cechy, których karty nie podają (kolor włosów Lumy, Zizzle'a, Orrina i Nixy, ubrania), dopisałem tak, żeby postacie łatwo było od siebie odróżnić. Jeśli gracz ma własną wizję, podmień je przed generowaniem.

---

## Styl wspólny (wklejaj na początku każdego promptu)

```text
Whimsical storybook fantasy illustration in the style of Pathfinder RPG art, painterly, rich detail, warm lantern light contrasting with deep teal underwater glow, humorous and cozy tone, expressive faces, gnomes are small humanoids about 3 feet tall with large bright eyes, big noses and vividly colored hair. No text, no letters, no watermark.
```

Wersja do portretów: dopisz na końcu `Half-body portrait, simple blurred background of an underwater magic academy.`

---

## Pip „Iskra” Torren

**Rdzeń:** włosy indygo przypalone na końcówkach, gogle na czole, amulet w kształcie klucza imbusowego.

```text
Pip, a young male gnome alarm technician and evocation wizard. Short spiky indigo hair singed and smoking at the tips, brass goggles pushed up on his forehead, an amulet shaped like a hex key on a chain, thick rubber insulating gloves, a utility vest covered with little alarm bells and buzzers, a battered notebook under his arm, a wand tucked behind his belt. Suspicious, slightly grumpy expression, eyebrows raised as if he just heard a false alarm.
```

**Portret z charakterem:** dopisz `He is holding a sandwich at arm's length with tongs, inspecting it for frogs.`

---

## Luma Bramblewick

**Rdzeń:** korona z srebrnej taśmy, rękawy w smarze, pas z narzędziami i klucz francuski.

```text
Luma, a young female gnome boiler-room scholar and transmutation wizard. Messy moss-green hair in a thick braid, a crown made of wrapped silver duct tape, rolled-up sleeves stained with grease, a heavy tool belt full of screwdrivers and bolts, a large masterwork adjustable wrench in one hand, a tin lunchbox with two sandwiches under her arm. Calm, confident half-smile, a smudge of soot on her cheek.
```

**Portret z charakterem:** dopisz `A tiny mischievous sprite in maintenance overalls peeks out of her lunchbox.`

---

## Zizzle Stormpocket

**Rdzeń:** broda narysowana kredą, malowane piegi, szarfa studenta z wymiany.

```text
Zizzle, a young male forest gnome exchange student and fey-blooded sorcerer. Wild sunflower-yellow hair with a few leaves and a tiny flower stuck in it, a clearly fake beard drawn on his chin with white chalk, painted freckles in uneven dots, a colorful exchange student sash across a forest-green tunic, pockets bulging with wrapped candies, faint shimmering fey sparkles around his fingers. Enormous, overly confident grin.
```

**Portret z charakterem:** dopisz `He proudly holds up a hand-written letter with a big official-looking stamp.`

---

## Orrin Quill

**Rdzeń:** palce w atramencie, kreda za uchem, książki pełne zakładek.

```text
Orrin, a young male gnome library assistant and divination wizard. Neat pale silver-blue hair tied in a short tail, a piece of chalk tucked behind one ear, ink-stained fingertips, an amulet with a clear crystal lens on his chest, a rolled blueprint sticking out of his coat pocket, a stack of books overflowing with paper bookmarks, a light crossbow slung on his back. Serious, slightly judgmental expression, one eyebrow raised over a red quill.
```

**Portret z charakterem:** dopisz `He is correcting someone's report with red ink, looking quietly delighted.`

---

## Nixa Embercoil

**Rdzeń:** miedziane piegi, połatana peleryna, amulet z zębem.

```text
Nixa, a young female gnome sorcerer of brass dragon blood. Flame-orange hair in a high messy bun, copper freckles across her nose and cheeks, a faint brass metallic sheen on her temples and knuckles, a long family cloak covered with silver duct tape patches and scorch marks, an amulet with a large yellowish tooth that is obviously a dog's tooth, small sparks drifting from her nostrils. Friendly but determined expression, one hand glowing with warm fire.
```

**Portret z charakterem:** dopisz `She clutches a thick sealed letter written in draconic script.`

## Wskazówki

- Generator myli postacie, gdy na obrazku jest ich więcej niż trzy. Przy plakacie drużyny ([przedstawienie.md](przedstawienie.md)) skróć opisy do rdzeni.
- Wszyscy bohaterowie są **gnomami**. Jeśli generator robi z nich ludzi albo krasnoludy, dopisz `tiny gnome, child-sized adult, no dwarf, no human`.
- Taśma Gnomoklej to po prostu `silver duct tape`.
